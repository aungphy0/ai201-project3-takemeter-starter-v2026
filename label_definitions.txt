# Label definitions for the LetsRun.com taxonomy - keep these identical to your README.
analysis: makes an argument where a specific, checkable fact (a time, date, stat, quote or named comparison) carries the argument
hot_take: a confident general claim about runners, races or the sport with no support offered
reaction: the writer's own response or experience - a feeling about a result or news that just happened, or a report of their own running - rather than an argument about the world
